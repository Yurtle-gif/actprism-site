FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

Is this legal advice?

No. ActPrism provides structured regulatory-readiness assessments based on the information you supply. We are not a law firm, and the report does not create a lawyer–client relationship. Where a question needs a legal opinion, the report flags it so you can take it to qualified counsel.

Does the AI Act apply to us if we're not based in the EU?

Very possibly. The Act covers providers that place AI systems on the EU market, and in some cases providers and deployers outside the EU whose AI output is used in the EU (Art. 2). The assessment confirms your position for each system.

Is everything enforceable now?

No, and you should be wary of anyone who says it is. Obligations apply in phases: prohibitions and AI literacy from 2 Feb 2025; general-purpose AI model rules from 2 Aug 2025; most other provisions, including Art. 50 transparency, from 2 Aug 2026; core high-risk requirements from 2 Dec 2027 (Annex III) or 2 Aug 2028 (Annex I), following Regulation (EU) 2026/1744. The report ties every action to the date that actually applies.

We just call an LLM API (OpenAI, Anthropic, etc.). Are we a "GPAI provider"?

Usually not. The general-purpose AI model obligations fall mainly on the companies that develop the models. If you build a product on top of one, you are typically the provider or deployer of an AI system, and the obligations that matter for you depend on what the system does. We map this for each feature.

Do we need a Fundamental Rights Impact Assessment (Art. 27)?

Most private SaaS companies don't. Art. 27 applies to public bodies, private entities providing public services, and deployers of certain credit-scoring and life/health-insurance pricing systems. Many companies still want a structured impact analysis, because customers ask for one and it supports risk management. Our report says clearly whether Art. 27 applies to you.

Templates, a fixed methodology, and AI-assisted drafting, followed by human review. You get consistency and speed. We don't skip the thinking.
